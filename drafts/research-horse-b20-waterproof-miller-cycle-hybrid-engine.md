## Verified facts (with sources)

- **B20 basics:** 2.0-liter turbocharged 4-cylinder, hybrid-first (HEV / PHEV / range-extender). Output 188-248 hp (190-252 PS) and 221-280 lb-ft (300-380 Nm) depending on spec. Weight 287 lbs (130 kg). (carscoops.com/2026/09/horse-waterproof-hybrid-engine/, carbuzz.com/horse-powertrain-waterproof-hybrid-engine-september-2026/, thamesgazette.com/technology/waterproof-engine-horse-b20/)
- **Waterproofing:** IPX8 rating; Horse claims continuous operation submerged in up to 42-43 in (1.1 m) of water. Fully sealed waterproof structure, torsional vibration dampers, dedicated engine-to-transmission sealing, IPX8-rated electrical components and sensors. (carbuzz, carscoops, thamesgazette)
- **Submersion response:** control system monitors environment via sensors; when submerged it automatically adjusts throttle response and/or limits power. (all three)
- **Gradeability:** 100% grade (45-degree) climbing capability claimed. (carscoops, carbuzz)
- **Thermal efficiency:** advanced Miller cycle, 48.4% brake thermal efficiency. Comparison context from carscoops: ~25-30% for most ICE engines, ~40% for Toyota Prius Miller-cycle engine.
- **Combustion hardware:** direct injection, high-tumble intake ports, specially designed pistons (cleaner, faster combustion event). (carbuzz)
- **Thermal/oil management:** fully active dual-cooling system; variable displacement oil pump. (thamesgazette)
- **Application:** first showcased at Beijing Auto Show 2026; fitted in Geely Galaxy Cruiser 700 off-road PHEV (China only), paired with HORSE 3DHT230 3-speed intelligent hybrid transmission. (carbuzz)
- **Company:** Horse Powertrain, founded 2024 by Renault and Geely. Manufactures engines for Mercedes-Benz and Renault (per carbuzz). Opened official US office in Southfield, Michigan on Sep 9, 2026 (carbuzz, published Sep 9 2026 6:14 PM EDT).

## IPX8 note (IEC 60529)
IPX8 = continuous immersion under conditions *specified by the manufacturer*; there is no fixed depth in the standard (IPX7 is 1 m / 30 min). Horse specified 1.1 m. This means the rating is honest only to the extent Horse's test protocol is transparent, a point worth making.

## Thermal efficiency context
- 48.4% BTE for a gasoline turbo four is in large marine/two-stroke diesel territory; best production gasoline engines (Toyota Dynamic Force 2.5L: ~40-41%) sit well below. It is a Horse claim, not independently verified on a third-party dyno.
- Miller cycle = late intake valve closing (LIVC): effective compression ratio lower than expansion ratio; the expansion stroke does more work. Turbocharged Miller pairs well because the turbo restores low-end air charge the LIVC sacrifices.

## Original angles (not in launch coverage)
1. **The engine as envelope, not output.** Suppliers historically sold horsepower; Horse sells an operating envelope (thermal + environmental tolerance). Waterproofing is the most auditable kind of engineering claim: you can literally drown-test it.
2. **Environment-state engine management.** Engines traditionally defend against internal states (knock, coolant temp). The B20 defends against an external environmental state, with the ECU derating on submersion detection. That is a genuine paradigm-adjacent shift worth examining (careful with banned phrase "paradigm shift" - do NOT use that phrase).
3. **The "Intel Inside" powertrain model.** Horse is a Renault-Geely JV that builds engines for Mercedes and is now opening a Michigan office. The supplier powertrain company is a real industry structure change: one combustion-engine architecture amortized across OEMs who have all stopped funding ICE R&D themselves.
4. **The caveat that matters:** a waterproof engine does not make a waterproof vehicle. Door seals, differential breathers, cabin electrics, air intake height all still limit fording. Horse claims the engine holds; the vehicle is someone else's problem.
5. **Gradeability honesty:** 100% (45 deg) grade is extraordinary; flag as Horse claim, unverified independently.

## Known unknowns / limitations
