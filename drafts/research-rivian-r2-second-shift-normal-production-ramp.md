# Research: Rivian R2 second-shift ramp at Normal, IL

## Topic
Rivian adding a second shift to the R2 assembly line at Normal, Illinois by end of Q3 2026 (Sept 30) — manufacturing engineering of the ramp, not the car.

## Angle
The site covered the R2's design-for-manufacturing in March (rivian-r2-engineering.html: wiring, ECUs, Maximus motors). This article is the second half of that story: whether the ramp itself works. Thesis: volume production is an engineering discipline, and the second shift is the proof the design held up. Anyone can build one car; the ramp is the test.

## Verified facts (Sept 14, 2026)
- Public customer deliveries began **June 9, 2026** (TechCrunch; Rivian Business Wire press release). Employee deliveries started April 2026.
- Second shift to be introduced **by end of Q3 (Sept 30)** at Normal, IL (InsideEVs, from Scaringe's investor call).
- Scaringe: "hundreds of suppliers are coordinating to bring up their own production" — ramp as a synchronization problem (InsideEVs).
- Rivian targets **20,000–25,000 R2 deliveries by end of 2026** (TechCrunch); "one of the fastest-scaling EV launches in U.S. history" if achieved.
- Normal expansion: **1.1M sq ft**; annual target **>150,000 R2s/year** at Normal (electrek, CarBuzz).
- Georgia factory comes online **late 2028** (InsideEVs).
- **EF-1 tornado struck Building 2 on the night of April 17**, collapsing a roof section; R2 operations paused for several days, then resumed; production start hit days later (electrek). Roof damage in the R2 operations area specifically.
- MVB (manufacturing validation builds) rolling off line in January 2026 (CarBuzz).
- R2 Performance Launch: 656 hp, dual-motor AWD, 330-mile EPA range, NACS charge port, semi-active suspension (InsideEVs/Carscoops).
- 200,000+ reservations; 57,000 demo drives; "significant number" of first-time EV buyers (InsideEVs).
- Rivian no longer discloses Normal headcount — new-worker count unknown.

## Context from earlier site coverage (rivian-r2-engineering.html, March 2026)
- Gen-2 architecture: wiring cut by 1.6 miles (InsideEVs, per Rivian), ECU count 17 to 7.
- Maximus motors: 91% fewer stator welds (240 welds removed).
- 4695-format battery cells; structural unibody.
- Use only as brief context; the March article owns that ground.

## Limitations
- Haven't been inside the Normal plant. Rivian discloses little about the line itself (no headcount, no takt time, no takt/cycle details).
- Supplier ramp coordination claim is from Scaringe's investor call; independent confirmation limited to the hiring news.
- State both explicitly in the article.
