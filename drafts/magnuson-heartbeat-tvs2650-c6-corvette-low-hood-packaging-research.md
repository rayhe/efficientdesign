# Research notes — Magnuson Heartbeat TVS2650 LS3 (#209, cars, Elena Voss)

## News hook
- Magnuson announced the Heartbeat 2650 LS3 on ~October 1, 2026 (GM Authority, 2 days ago at research time).
- First kits: late October 2026, for LS3-powered hot rods with the Corvette accessory-drive configuration.
- Complete C6 Corvette kit: planned later in 2026.
- Positioning: larger TVS2650 rotor pack in the same low-profile Heartbeat housing that fits under a factory C6 hood. More compact than Magnuson's existing Magnum LS3 TVS2650.
- Magnuson claims 1,200+ hp support with supporting mods (built engine, fuel system, tuning).

## Technical facts (all sourced)
- Rotor pack: Eaton TVS2650, 2.65 liters displacement per revolution, high-helix four-lobe rotors.
- Rotor capability (fenced as rotor-pack rating, from Texas Speed universal TVS2650R kit page): up to 24,000 rpm, 2.4 pressure ratio.
- Heartbeat architecture (PRI 2014, LSX Mag): air forced FORWARD (not downward into the manifold like traditional top-mount Maggies); split twin intercooler "bricks" angled mid-way between blower outlet and cylinder heads; charge air flows horizontally instead of vertically; dimples inside coolant cores tumble coolant for heat transfer; demand-operated vacuum bypass unloads the blower at idle/light throttle (NA-like cruise economy) and recirculates intercooled air for rapid cooldown.
- Original Heartbeat 2300: 1-7/8 in taller than a stock LS3 manifold, fits under stock C6 hood (Matt Hatley quote); +25 hp and 17 lb lighter vs Magnuson's previous modular kit.
- LSX Mag on the tradeoff: "The Heartbeat puts its angled intercoolers mid-way between the blower outlet and the cylinder heads, trading a bit of extra height compared to the LT4 in exchange for more plenum volume, and room for bigger rotors."
- GM's LT4 (C7 Z06) counter-approach: minimized height at all costs. TVS R1740 (1.7 L/rev) instead of LS9's 2.3 L. Rotor dims 7.9 in long x 3.9 in dia vs LS9's 8.3 x 4.4. Driven 3.10:1 vs LS9's 2.32:1 to compensate.
- Two charge-air coolers on the 2650 (per GM Authority).
- Universal-kit drivetrain details (Texas Speed): jackshaft drive, high-torque/low-inertia carbon fiber driveshaft, quick-change cartridge pulleys (32/34-tooth cogs standard, 30-38 available), press-on hub for 2-piece pulley; high-flow inlet for electronic throttles w/ GM LT5 bolt pattern, fits Magnuson 109 mm mechanical TB; port-injection fuel rails (1/2 in thru bore), injectors not included.
- C6 context: LS3 6.2L from 2008; famously low hood line; owners resist aftermarket cowl hoods (paint match).

## Original contributions (theses)
1. The three-way trade: rotor size vs plenum volume vs hood height. Magnuson spends its height budget on intercoolers (angled, mid-position) and keeps rotors big; GM's LT4 spent the budget on neither and shrank the rotors instead, spinning them 3.10:1. Magnuson's is the better compromise.
2. The bypass valve is the quiet technology: it is what makes a 1,200-hp-capable Roots blower street-livable (cruise economy of an NA engine, instant boost on demand).
3. 1,200 hp is a statement about the engine, not the blower: the blower is no longer the bottleneck; the LS3's bottom end is.
4. Why hot-rod kits ship first: the Corvette accessory drive is the common denominator; the full C6 kit needs vehicle-specific plumbing and heat-exchanger placement.
