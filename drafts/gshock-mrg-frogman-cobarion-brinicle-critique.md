### 1. Technical Accuracy — 9.0/10
Core claims verified: COBARION is a cobalt-chromium alloy manufactured by Eiwa Corporation, approximately 4x harder than pure titanium (confirmed by Casio's official MR-G materials page). Work-hardening property under mechanical stress is confirmed by G-SHOCK Pillars of Protection Vol. 4 and Professor Chiba's research context. Nitrogen injection during melting increasing hardness is documented. COBARION's platinum-like gleam (comparable to 950 platinum in brightness, hue, saturation) is from Casio's own specification. Kazuhito Komatsu's role confirmed via Revolution Watch interview (2021 Hana-Basara piece). MRG-BF1000EB specs (800 pieces, COBARION bezel, blue AIP, 57-facet lab-grown blue sapphires, brinicle theme, ISO 200m, two bands, Proteca collaboration box) all confirmed from Casio's May 27, 2026 press release. The Clad Guard construction, fluoro rubber buffers, and Multi-Band 6 radio sync via sapphire case back are documented on Casio's MRG-BF1000 product pages. Minor: the exact Vickers hardness of COBARION is not publicly disclosed by Eiwa, so the article wisely avoids citing a specific number, relying on the "4x titanium" comparative. MR-G launch date of 1996 is correct (first model MRG-100).
