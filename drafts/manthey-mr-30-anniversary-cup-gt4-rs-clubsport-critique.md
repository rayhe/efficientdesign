### Engineering Depth: 9.0/10
The bore-versus-boost argument is made at mechanism level: displacement lifts the whole torque curve without lag, heat, or a turbocharger to keep alive, versus the modern reflex of adding boost. The costs of boring a stressed racing flat-six are stated honestly (longer flame travel, heavier pistons, thinner cylinder walls). The validation-at-a-deficit read of the 7:58.605 lap (chassis/aero/cooling proven before final power) is the article's sharpest engineering observation. The central radiator is explained through Manthey's stated design logic (contact protection, cleaner exhaust airflow) with its decade-long lineage noted. The recycled-carbon paragraph is a genuine materials-science contribution: reclaimed fiber's property scatter versus virgin continuous layups, and what designing for statistical variance means on a race aero package. Deductions: no downforce figures were published so the aero discussion stays qualitative; no damper architecture detail beyond six-way adjustability; the asymmetric track widening (+20/+50) is reported but its roll-stiffness implication is left implicit.
