# Critique — Article #120: Grand Seiko 9SA5 Dual Impulse Escapement

## Critic 1: Technical Accuracy (Horological Engineering)
**Score: 9.0/10**
Strong technical foundation. Accurate description of dual impulse mechanics, MEMS fabrication, and the relationship between escapement efficiency and power reserve. The comparison with Omega's co-axial is fair and well-nuanced. Minor: the article states "more than 200 proposed alternatives" citing general horological literature — this figure comes from George Daniels' own writing and is defensible. The distinction between sliding friction in one path versus friction-free in the other is correctly handled. The "half the friction" claim is directionally correct but slightly simplified — actual friction reduction depends on impulse duration ratios and contact geometries, not a clean 50/50 split.

## Critic 2: Prose Quality & Voice
**Score: 9.0/10**
Clean, authoritative prose. Good sentence variety — short declarative statements alternate with longer explanatory passages. Zero em dashes. No AI slop. Opening is strong: positioning the article around the historical exclusivity of the achievement rather than just "Grand Seiko made a new movement" gives it immediate editorial weight. The "What It Does Not Solve" section is a standout — honest criticism of the subject elevates the entire piece. Only nit: "Perhaps more significant in the long run" is slightly hedging for this voice.
