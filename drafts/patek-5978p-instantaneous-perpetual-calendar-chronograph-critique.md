### Engineering Depth: 9.2/10
The central thesis, that variable charging duration makes torque demand independent of month length, is argued at mechanism level: spring deflection over fixed vs variable windows, the February 28 four-position problem, balance-amplitude consequences of load changes, and why the grand-lever/notch/drive-lever train implements the schedule. The roller-mounted date ring with rear-face teeth is read as a packaging decision that buys numeral size. The curved reset slot is presented as a failure-mode fix (hand breakage on counterclockwise reset), which is the correct engineering register for a detail most launch coverage skipped. The 18-year module history is given a causal reading (repeaters as the proving ground for energy discipline) rather than a timeline. Minor deduction: no amplitude-trace data exists publicly to quantify the flat-torque claim, and the article correctly labels it as mechanism consequence rather than measurement.
