### Critic 1: Technical Accuracy (10/10)
The article correctly explains the bistable blade mechanism, the distinction between constant-force escapements and upstream torque-equalizing devices (fusée-and-chain, remontoir), the DRIE fabrication process, and the Neo revision's co-axial escape wheel redesign. Specifications (14μm blade width, 120μm thickness, 266 components, 29 jewels, 21,600 vph, 7-day reserve, 30 blades per wafer vs 500 hairsprings) all match published sources from Hodinkee, Monochrome Watches, Revolution Watch, and Swiss Watches Magazine. The historical timeline (Mudge 1770s, Breguet 1798, Déhon 1990s, GP 2008/2013/2023) is accurate. The comparison with Breguet's magnetic escapement and FP Journe's remontoir correctly characterizes where each acts in the power delivery chain. No technical errors found.
