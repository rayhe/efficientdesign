# Research: Lamborghini Temerario Spyder — first electric retractable hard top

## Topic
Lamborghini Temerario Spyder, unveiled November 19, 2026 (Sant'Agata Bolognese and Los Angeles, Academy Museum of Motion Pictures). First retractable hard top ever fitted to a Lamborghini Spyder, and the first fully electric one: patented vertical stow, ten electric actuators, no electro-hydraulic circuitry.

## Angle
The roof is the story, not the horsepower. A mid-engine supercar convertible has always paid for open-air driving in stiffness, weight, or balance. Lamborghini claims this one pays almost nothing: the roof stands up behind the seats instead of lying flat, and the car gets lower and no heavier at the axles when open. Thesis: a hard top that disappears vertically is a packaging solution first and a roof second, and the spaceframe numbers are the receipt. The 10,000-rpm V8 is the context, not the headline.

## Verified facts (Nov 19-25, 2026)
- Unveiled **November 19, 2026**, Sant'Agata Bolognese and Los Angeles (Academy Museum of Motion Pictures). Marks 50 years of open-top series-production Lamborghinis, traced to the 1976 Silhouette. (Lamborghini newsroom, Nov 19)
- **First retractable hard top on a Lamborghini Spyder.** Fully electric, patented vertical stowed position, ten electric actuators, eliminates traditional electro-hydraulic circuitry. (Lamborghini newsroom)
- Stowing vertically behind the seats **lowers the centre of gravity by 3 mm** when open vs closed, weight distribution unaltered. (Lamborghini newsroom)
- Roof cycle **~17 seconds, operable up to 50 km/h (31 mph)**. (Hypebeast Nov 19; Gearculture)
- Roof is two panels that stow vertically behind the seats. Electronically adjustable rear window modulates airflow and engine sound. (Lamborghini newsroom; Ben Rental Car)
- **Spaceframe (all-aluminium): +6% static torsional rigidity, +9% dynamic bending stiffness, +6% dynamic torsional rigidity vs Huracan Spyder.** Structural components cut 50%, heat-input welds cut 80% vs Huracan EVO Spyder. (Lamborghini newsroom)
- **HPEV powertrain:** 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8, 800 CV (588 kW) alone, revs to 10,000 rpm, 10,250 rpm with Launch Control. Three axial-flux electric motors (one rear, two front axle). Combined **920 CV (677 kW / 907 hp), 800 Nm**. 0-100 km/h 2.8 s, 0-200 km/h 7.3 s, top speed 343 km/h (213 mph). (Lamborghini newsroom)
- V8 technical highlights: **180-degree flat-plane crankshaft**, twin turbos up to **2.5 bar** boost inside the 90-degree V (hot-V), titanium connecting rods, A357+Cu aluminium cylinder heads, DLC-coated finger followers. Highest engine speed of any series-production open-top super sports car with a turbocharged engine, per Lamborghini. (Lamborghini newsroom; Bridgestone release)
- **13 selectable driving experiences** combining Citta, Strada, Sport, Corsa, Corsa ESC Off with Recharge, Hybrid, Performance strategies. Citta electric-only up to 190 CV (140 kW). Three-level Drift Mode. (Lamborghini newsroom)
- Cabin vs Huracan EVO Spyder: **+46 mm legroom, +22 mm headroom**, +9 mm shoulder/elbow room; fits occupants up to 2.0 m. Three-level seat neck warmer. **112-litre front luggage compartment.** (Vulkan Magazine; Ben Rental Car)
- **Dry weight ~1,805 kg** (Gearculture), about 100 kg (220 lb) more than the coupe at 1,690 kg dry (Jalopnik via Batmangarage). Lamborghini's own release gives no weight figure.
- Optional **Akrapovic titanium exhaust: 11.40 kg, 12% lighter** than standard, black tailpipes. Optional **Alleggerita package saves up to ~22 kg** (CFRP parts + carbon wheels). (Vulkan; Batmangarage)
- Bridgestone exclusive tire partner; same Potenza Sport fitment as the coupe. (Bridgestone Americas, Nov 19)
- **No price announced** by Lamborghini at launch; no delivery date in the official release. Reported US figures ($390,900) are press reports, not official: do NOT use as a fact. Site rule: no pricing coverage anyway.
- Apple Vision Pro experience expanded with the Spyder (Nov 20 newsroom item) — out of scope for this article.

## Context / comparisons
- Huracan EVO Spyder (predecessor): naturally aspirated V10, soft top, no hard-top option in 50 years of open-top series production.
- Aston Martin Valhalla Spider covered Oct 28 on this site (different car, different roof logic); Ferrari 849 Testarossa covered Nov 11 (FIVE digital twin, different thesis). No Temerario coverage on this site yet (coupe or Spyder).
- The vertical-stow idea inverts the usual convertible compromise: most retractable hard tops stack horizontally over the engine or in the trunk, raising mass and stealing luggage space. Here the roof becomes a vertical slab behind the seats, which is why the CG claim and the unchanged weight distribution are physically plausible.

## Original contributions to develop
- Actuator-count arithmetic: ten electric actuators vs one hydraulic pump plus lines, valves, and fluid. What electric actuation buys (per-actuator position control, no leak path, no fluid mass) and what it costs (ten motors to fail, ten controllers to synchronize).
- The 3 mm CG claim: small in absolute terms, but directionally the opposite of every other folding hard top, which raises CG when stowed. Frame it as the sign the packaging worked, not as a handling revolution.
- Spaceframe weld reduction (80% fewer heat-input welds): heat-affected zones are where aluminium spaceframes lose strength; fewer welds is a stiffness story, not a cost story.
- The V8's 10,000 rpm with turbos: forced induction and high revs usually fight each other (turbo sizing, exhaust backpressure, valve-train mass). DLC finger followers + titanium rods + flat-plane crank are the three answers, in that order.
- Honest limit: Lamborghini publishes no Spyder weight in its own release, and the 1,805 kg figure is a secondary-source number. State that plainly.

## Limitations
- Not driven, not handled. No independent measurements (all figures are Lamborghini's or secondary reports).
- No price, no delivery date, no official weight in the manufacturer's release.
- Roof durability, actuator service life, and real-world noise with the top up are unanswerable until cars reach customers (spring 2027 per press reports, not official).

## What to avoid
- Pricing/markup coverage (Ray rule): no price analysis. Mention only that Lamborghini announced no price.
- Do not present the reported $390,900 US price as fact.
- Do not rehash the Temerario coupe at length; the coupe is context, the roof is the article.
