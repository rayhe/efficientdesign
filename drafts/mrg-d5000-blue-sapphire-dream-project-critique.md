## Scores

### Technical Accuracy: 8.5/10
Core facts (price ¥11,000,000/$80,000/€80,000, 20 pieces, lineage, Multi-Guard structure, full spec table, allocation mechanics, sibling lineup) all match sourced launch reporting (Casio PR via PRNewswire APAC, G-Central, Hypebeast, Notebookcheck, watchdavid, MensGear, The Gadgeteer). One genuine factual error caught and fixed during critique: sapphire (3.98 g/cm³) is slightly LESS dense than Grade 5 titanium (4.43), not "barely denser." Also fixed: "no screws at all" softened to bezel/case assembly (caseback is a threaded titanium ring, clarified in text); "most expensive G-Shock in the brand's history" qualified to "most expensive G-Shock it has ever offered for sale"; D= Dream Project and Blue Rose Project attributions sourced (MensGear/Casio). Hedging discipline strong throughout ("per the October 1 release," "By Casio's claim," "the obvious theory"). The materials framing (Mohs 9, ~2 MPa·m^1/2 vs ~20x for Ti-6Al-4V, Fe/Ti dopants, diamond machining) is accurate standard engineering knowledge with an explicit honesty blockquote.

### Engineering Depth: 8.5/10
The hardness-vs-toughness thesis is argued at mechanism level: fracture toughness asymmetry, screw holes as crack initiation sites in brittle ceramics, no plastic deformation to blunt crack tips. Timber-joinery load-path argument coherent (bearing across contact areas vs threaded discontinuities); 2022 titanium MRG-B5000 read as proof-in-a-forgiving-material is a sound original inference. Critique additions raised it: subsurface machining damage as the unspoken enemy, Weibull size effect (smaller stressed volumes are statistically stronger, a second independent reason for multi-piece), Hublot MP-11 Magic Sapphire / Big Bang Unico Sapphire named as the tried-and-fragile thick-sapphire foil, sealing path (gaskets vs titanium inner case) flagged as unanswered, and the Bluetooth omission given a competing antenna/shielding hypothesis alongside the budget theory. Missing: nothing structural; the joint-face contact question (lapped hard contact vs compliant layer) and corner-stress-riser trade are now asked explicitly.
