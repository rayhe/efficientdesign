## News hook (fresh, Oct 2026)
- GM service bulletin dated Oct 8, 2026: Customer Satisfaction Program N262573420. 11 units of 2026 CT5-V Blackwing with optional Precision Package delivered WITHOUT the rear suspension toe links included in the package. Dealers to supply part 85829248 (two rear toe-link assemblies) + 4 mounting bolts; free through Oct 31, 2028. Owners notified by mail. (gmauthority.com, published 2026-10-26)
- The missing parts are the Precision Package's aluminum performance toe links, which replace factory steel components and allow more aggressive rear-wheel alignment (track).

- UNIQUE BILLET ALUMINUM rear toe links enabling more aggressive track alignment
- Recalibrated Magnetic Ride Control, revised steering/chassis calibrations, eLSD adjustments, carbon ceramic brakes

## Engineering angle (original contribution)
- What a toe link does: locates the rear wheel fore-aft, sets static toe; under cornering/braking loads, compliance in the link lets the wheel steer itself (compliance steer / bump steer). Stiffness of the link = how much the toe setting survives load. Machined billet aluminum vs stamped/welded steel: tighter tolerances, higher specific stiffness, lower unsprung mass.
- Why it matters that GM issued a satisfaction program for 11 missing links: the part exists at all because the Precision Package's alignment window only works with the stiffer link; a steel link under the same alignment would deflect. The bulletin is a statement of engineering intent.
- The crossover to watchmaking: hand-built engines with a builder's plaque are the "one man, one engine" AMG philosophy; GM's version puts the name where the customer can see it.
- Contrast with M5: hybrid, heavier, chasing benchmarks. Roma's point: the Blackwing optimizes for the driver, not the comparison test.
