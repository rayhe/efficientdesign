### Engineering Depth: 9.0/10
The central thesis, that the redesign is a case-engineering project rather than a cosmetic refresh, is argued at mechanism level: steel-cradle-plus-insert bezel construction analyzed through impact-energy behavior (plastic deformation vs brittle fracture), insert serviceability vs monolithic ring replacement cost, and the claim that part of the 1.31mm thickness reduction comes from the insert no longer needing self-supporting wall thickness. Diameter/area/volume scaling argument is explicit about its approximations. Vertical-clutch no-jump engagement vs horizontal-clutch friction explained correctly as the reason the carryover movement is the right call. Dial legibility section engages the actual tradeoff (printed numerals vs faceted batons at speed, wrist-angle consistency) rather than asserting taste. Deduction: no published barrel-torque or pusher-force figures exist for the TH20-00 in this case, and the article correctly declines to invent them, but the 80-hour reserve is taken at brochure value with no timing-state qualification.
